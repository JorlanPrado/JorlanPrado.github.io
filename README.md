Showcase portfolio website for video editing and content repurposing, featuring vertical short-form edits and full video archive.

- **Proportioned 9:16 Video Showcase**: Real video clips with playback controls, progress bars, and mute options.
- **Showcased Clips**:

- **Resume & Cover Letter**: Built-in resume and cover letter modals.
